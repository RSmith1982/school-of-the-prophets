3. Courses and Examinations

A course is complete when its assessment is passed (80%; 90% with honors) and the Bishop has approved it.

4. Practical Training

Each activity of a level is completed in parts, in order: receive live training on the activity, witness the activity completed by an approved instructor, and perform the activity under supervision. The Pastor and Bishop diplomas add a fourth part: successfully teach someone else the activity. Press a part to report it done, with a note to the Bishop; he approves each one. The course pages for each level are listed under Courses above.
