How a Course Ends

Finishing the reading is not the end. Each course closes with an examination that shows exactly what you understood and what you did not.

  1. 1

    Sit the Examination

    Answer a set of questions drawn from the course readings. The passing standard is 80%.

  2. 2

    Review Your Assessment

    See every question marked correct or incorrect, with a breakdown of your score by topic so you know where to return.

  3. 3

    Receive Your Certificate

    Pass, and a formal certificate of completion is issued in your name with your score and the date.

Practical Training

Knowledge alone does not make a minister. Every diploma includes hands-on training in the activities of ministry, each one completed in parts, in order, and approved step by step by the Bishop. The first three parts are required for every diploma; the fourth is required for the Pastor and Bishop diplomas.

Part One

Receive Live Training

Receive live training on the activity from an approved instructor: what it is, why the Church does it, and how it is done well.

Part Two

Witness the Activity

Witness the activity completed by an approved instructor in a real setting, and reflect on what you saw.

Part Three

Perform Under Supervision

Perform the activity yourself under the supervision of an approved instructor, who reports to the Bishop.

Part Four — Pastor and Bishop

Teach Someone Else

Successfully teach someone else the activity, so that the practice is carried on through you.
